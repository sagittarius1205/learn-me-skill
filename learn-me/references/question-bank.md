# Question Bank

These are raw material, not a script. Choose what fits the moment, reword it in your own voice and the user's language, and react to the answer before asking the next one. Every question is skippable.

Each area runs from **light** (fine at any time) to **deep** (ask only once there's some trust and the user seems open).

## Basics

- *Light:* What should I call you?
- *Light:* What language do you like to chat in?
- *Light:* What does a normal weekday look like for you?
- *Light:* Where in the world are you, roughly? (Helpful for time zones, seasons, holidays.)
- *Medium:* What chapter of life are you in right now? Studying, building a career, raising kids, starting over, enjoying retirement…?

## Thoughts & ideas

- *Light:* What's been on your mind lately?
- *Light:* Any idea you keep coming back to, even a half-baked one?
- *Medium:* What's something you believe that most people around you don't?
- *Medium:* If you had a free month to work on any project, what would it be?
- *Deep:* What's a question you've been chewing on for years?

## Favorites

- *Light:* What's your go-to comfort food?
- *Light:* What are you watching, reading or listening to these days?
- *Light:* What's a small thing that reliably makes your day better?
- *Light:* Favorite way to spend a free afternoon?
- *Medium:* Is there a place that feels like *yours*, like a café, a trail or a corner of your home?
- *Medium:* Which book, film or song changed how you see things?

## Please avoid

These have the biggest payoff for trust. Ask them early, matter-of-factly.

- *Light:* Is there anything AI assistants do that drives you up the wall?
- *Light:* Any words, phrases or tones that make you cringe?
- *Light:* Anything you'd rather I *not* do? Too many emoji, too much cheerleading, long intros, lecturing…?
- *Medium:* Are there topics you'd rather I steer clear of unless you bring them up?
- *Medium:* When you're stressed, what's the *least* helpful thing someone can say?

## Communication style

- *Light:* Do you prefer short and punchy, or detailed and thorough?
- *Light:* Bullet points or normal paragraphs?
- *Light:* Casual or more professional?
- *Light:* Are jokes welcome? What kind of humor do you like?
- *Medium:* When your idea has a flaw, how blunt should I be? Gentle, straight, or "rip it apart"?
- *Medium:* When you're learning something, do you want the answer, the reasoning, or a nudge so you figure it out yourself?

## How they work

- *Light:* What do you do, for work, study, or whatever fills your days?
- *Light:* Morning person, night owl, or depends on the day?
- *Light:* Which tools or apps do you live in?
- *Medium:* Are you a planner, or more of an "I'll figure it out as I go" person?
- *Medium:* What helps you focus? What kills your focus?
- *Medium:* How do you learn best: reading, watching, doing, or talking it through?
- *Deep:* When does work feel *great* to you? What's happening in those moments?

## Their people

Remember names; using them later is one of the warmest things you can do.

- *Light:* Who do you see or talk to most days?
- *Light:* Any pets? (Always ask the pet's name.)
- *Medium:* Who's on your team, at work or in life?
- *Medium:* Who do you go to when you need advice? When you need a laugh?
- *Medium:* Anyone you're trying to help, support or take care of right now?
- *Deep:* Is there a relationship you're working on, or wish were different?

## What they have on hand

Advice is only useful if it fits what someone actually has.

- *Light:* What devices and software do you usually work with?
- *Light:* How much free time do you realistically have in a week?
- *Medium:* What skills do you already have that you might be underrating?
- *Medium:* Who could you ask for help with your goals: friends, family, communities, colleagues?
- *Medium:* Are you working with a budget for this, or is it more of a zero-cost situation? (Ask only when it matters for a goal, and don't ask for exact figures.)
- *Medium:* What's already half-done or sitting in a drawer that could be picked back up?

## Goals

- *Light:* What are you hoping to get done this week?
- *Medium:* What would make this year a great one for you?
- *Medium:* What are you working toward that you're excited about?
- *Medium:* What's the first step you haven't taken yet? What's in the way?
- *Deep:* Picture yourself five years from now and things went really well. What does your life look like?

When a goal comes up, gently learn: *why* it matters to them, *when* they'd like to get there, and *what the next step is*.

## Wish list

- *Light:* What's on your someday list?
- *Light:* Where would you go if you could travel anywhere next month?
- *Light:* What's a skill you'd love to learn just for fun?
- *Medium:* Something you'd love to make or build with your own hands?
- *Medium:* Is there an experience you've always wanted to have but keep putting off?
- *Deep:* If you knew you couldn't fail, what would you try?

## Values & drivers

- *Medium:* What does a really good day look like for you?
- *Medium:* What gets you out of bed when things are hard?
- *Medium:* What do you want people to say about you when you're not in the room?
- *Deep:* What matters most to you right now, and has that changed over the years?

## Challenges

Approach gently. Listening comes before fixing.

- *Medium:* What's the hardest part of your life right now? (You can say "pass".)
- *Medium:* What keeps you up at night, if anything?
- *Medium:* Is there something you've been avoiding?
- *Deep:* What's a fear you'd like to be braver about?

## Wins & strengths

This is ammunition for encouragement later. Collect it generously.

- *Light:* What's something you did recently that you're quietly proud of?
- *Light:* What do friends usually come to you for?
- *Medium:* What's something hard you got through that you didn't think you could?
- *Medium:* What compliment has stuck with you over the years?

## Technology & AI

Listen without judgment. Excitement and worry are both reasonable.

- *Light:* How do you feel about tech in general: excited, comfortable, overwhelmed, somewhere in between?
- *Light:* What do you already use AI for, if anything?
- *Medium:* Is there something about AI or the future that worries you?
- *Medium:* If tech could take one annoying thing off your plate, what would it be?
- *Medium:* Is there something you've wanted to learn (coding, design, video, data…) but thought was "not for you"?
- *Deep:* How do you hope your work or life looks in a world with a lot more AI in it?

## How they want you to be

- *Light:* What kind of helper do you want me to be: friend, coach, cheerleader, sparring partner, or quiet assistant?
- *Light:* Do you want me to check in about your goals now and then, or only when you bring them up?
- *Medium:* When you're having a rough day, what would actually help? Distraction, a pep talk, a plan, or just someone to listen?
- *Medium:* Should I nudge you when you're being too hard on yourself?

## Just for fun

Good for warming up, lightening the mood, or a short "getting to know you" moment between tasks.

- What's your unpopular food opinion?
- Which fictional world would you live in?
- What's the best thing you've ever eaten while traveling?
- What were you obsessed with as a kid?
- What's a hill you'll happily die on?
- If your life had a theme song this month, what would it be?

## Micro-questions for everyday work

One of these, asked at the right moment during a real task, often beats a whole interview. Never more than one per task, and only when the answer would change how you help.

- Is this for work, school, or something personal? (Only if it changes the help.)
- Do you want just the answer, or the reasoning too?
- Is this level of detail about right, or should I go shorter or longer?
- Is this part of something bigger you're working on?
- Do you have a deadline on this?
- Who's going to read this? (Helps tone, and often reveals a person worth remembering.)
- Was that helpful, or did I miss the mark?

## Good follow-ups

These turn an answer into a conversation:

- "What do you love about it?"
- "How did you get into that?"
- "What's that like day to day?"
- "What would it mean to you if that worked out?"
- "What's stopping you, honestly?"
- "Tell me more about [person/thing they mentioned]."
- Reflect back and check: "So it sounds like ___. Is that right?"
